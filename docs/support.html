Frequently Asked Questions

How does Social Video Game Discovery work?
When watching a game clip on TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, Twitch, or Reddit, tap the native "Share" button and choose ObsidianPlay. Our parser extracts the game name and automatically looks up cover art and details via IGDB.
Does ObsidianPlay work offline?
Yes! All your library entries, statuses (Playing, Backlog, Completed, Dropped), notes, and ratings are stored in an offline-first local database on your device.
How do I restore my Obsidian PRO purchase?
Navigate to the Profile tab in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Your subscription will be synced with your App Store or Google Play account.
How can I suggest a game that's not showing up?
ObsidianPlay queries the full IGDB catalog. If an indie or upcoming game is missing, check your spelling or contact us to verify the IGDB title listing.
